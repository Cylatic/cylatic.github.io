RESEARCH STANDARD

Research built for defenders.

Cylatic Research turns current security developments into technically useful defensive analysis. The objective is to help practitioners understand exposure, telemetry, detection and remediation—not simply repeat the news.

Methodology

From event to engineering decision.

Research topics are selected from current security developments and evaluated for technical significance, affected technologies, defensive relevance and the amount of original analysis Cylatic can contribute.

01

Source and verify

We use reputable security advisories, vendor disclosures, incident reporting and other primary or high-quality sources. Material claims should be attributable and checked before publication.

02

Add technical value

Articles focus on attack surface, prerequisites, telemetry, detection engineering, threat hunting, containment, hardening and operational implications.

03

Use AI as an engineering assistant

Automation can assist with research synthesis and drafting. It is not treated as an authority. Generated material is constrained toward defensive analysis and should not invent sources, incidents, CVEs or technical facts.

04

Prefer substance over volume

The automated pipeline can monitor frequently, but publication is gated by novelty, significance and technical value. Quiet periods should produce no article rather than filler.

05

Correct errors

If a material factual or technical error is identified, Cylatic may update the article and its publication metadata. Readers can report corrections through contact@cylatic.com.

Safety boundary

Defensive research, not unauthorized access.

Cylatic may discuss vulnerabilities, attack techniques and detection logic for legitimate defensive purposes, but research is not intended to facilitate unauthorized access, credential theft, malware deployment or evasion of security controls.
