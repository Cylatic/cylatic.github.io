Privacy Policy

Cylatic operates this website and its browser-based cybersecurity and developer tools. We aim to collect only information needed to operate, secure and improve the website.

Information and tools

Many Cylatic tools process input directly in your browser. Where a tool does not require a server request, the entered value is not intentionally transmitted to Cylatic. Users should still avoid entering passwords, private keys, authentication tokens, credentials or other sensitive production data into online tools.

Contact enquiries

If you submit a contact enquiry, Cylatic receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company and message, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and providing requested services. Do not submit secrets, credentials, API keys, private keys or sensitive customer information through the website form.

Advertising and cookies

Cylatic may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize or measure advertising and to provide, maintain and improve advertising services, subject to applicable law and the consent choices available to visitors.

European users

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers using personalized advertising to use an appropriate certified consent management platform and obtain consent where required. Cylatic will use Google's available privacy and messaging controls for applicable advertising traffic.

Analytics and logs

Basic technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or advertising providers to operate the website, prevent abuse, understand usage and improve performance. Such processing may include technical information such as browser, device, request and diagnostic information.

Third-party services

This website may link to or load third-party services such as Google advertising, Google Fonts, Font Awesome CDN resources and the contact-form delivery provider. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. When you submit a contact form, the form delivery provider processes the submitted information to deliver the enquiry to Cylatic.

Security

We take reasonable technical and operational measures to protect information handled through the website. No internet transmission or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention

Contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can control browser cookies and relevant advertising preferences through your browser and applicable Google privacy controls. For privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted directly to Cylatic, contact contact@cylatic.com.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website, services, advertising configuration or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
