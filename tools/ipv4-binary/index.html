Convert an IPv4 address into binary, hexadecimal and decimal formats for networking, troubleshooting and learning.

Why convert IPv4 to binary?

IPv4 addresses are 32-bit values. Binary representation makes subnet masks, network bits and host bits easier to understand during network engineering and troubleshooting.
